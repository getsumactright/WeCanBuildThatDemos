Newest direction · Round four

10 / Dark editorial · Burnt orange

The Upgrade

Night olive, khaki and burnt orange in Host Grotesk, after the stylist template you shared. Marcus stands in front of a giant "THE MVP" headline; his photos sit in duotone and bloom into colour as you explore his roles, the plays and the lookbook.

Best for: the most striking first impression, built around Marcus himself and his favourite colour.

07 / Game-night programme

Four Quarters

Espresso, hardwood and ball orange in Anton and JetBrains Mono. Each package is drawn as the basketball play it is named after, and the chalkboard runs the play.

Best for: the strongest expression of the point-guard story, still tailoring-first.

08 / The mirror

See It. Believe It.

Aubergine, lilac and brass in Gloock and Figtree. Built on Marcus’s mirror quote: a sticky mirror changes reflection as you scroll, then a clear package comparison.

Best for: a warm, premium feel that stays approachable.

09 / Fashion magazine

The Cover Story

Newsprint, ink and military olive in Rozha One, Libre Franklin and Cormorant italic. The site is an issue of a men's fashion magazine: a cover you can switch between three editions, a contents page, a feature interview and a lookbook spread.

Best for: the most glamorous, fashion-first presentation of Marcus.
