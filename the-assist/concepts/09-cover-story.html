The cover story · p. 04

The man who passes you the ball.

Marcus Ervin on self-confidence, the mirror, and why a point guard makes the best style coach.

In his own wordsMeet Marcus Ervin

From a young age, like many of us, I battled with the occasional self-confidence issues and insecurities. As I grew older, I began to realize that my style and clothing gave me a great head start in feeling better about myself and my image.

The more I began to learn about permanent fashion and style, the more I realized the power of, first, projecting confidence to myself…and then that confidence would become evident to others.

“When you see it, you will begin to believe it.”

So, that is what has motivated me to share what I have learned with you. I have read many books and taken hours upon hours of instructional classes to educate myself and build up a wealth of knowledge to assist you.

As a huge basketball fan and a lover of some of the game's premier point guards, my aim is similar: to pass you the ball and facilitate your success. My purpose is to make you see the best version of yourself when you look in the mirror. And when you believe it, inevitably so will others!
