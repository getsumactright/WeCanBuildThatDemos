From a young age, like many of us, I battled with the occasional self-confidence issues and insecurities. As I grew older, I began to realize that my style and clothing gave me a great head start in feeling better about myself and my image.

Read Marcus's story

The more I began to learn about permanent fashion and style, the more I realized the power of, first, projecting confidence to myself…and then that confidence would become evident to others.

I have read many books and taken hours upon hours of instructional classes to educate myself and build up a wealth of knowledge to assist you. As a huge basketball fan and a lover of some of the game's premier point guards, my aim is similar: to pass you the ball and facilitate your success.
