The Stylist Behind The Assist

“Think of me as your style coach, trainer, and barber — all in one.”

As I grew older, I began to realize that my style and clothing gave me a great head start in feeling better about myself and my image.

As a huge basketball fan and a lover of some of the game’s premier point guards, my aim is similar: to pass you the ball and facilitate your success.

My purpose is to make you see the best version of yourself when you look in the mirror…because when you see it, you will begin to believe it.

Intentional

Every lapel width, texture contrast, and shoe choice selected with purpose.

Personal

Crafted directly around your posture, skin tones, and daily physical reality.
